# dirvec session 8: the modality gap at folder level

Question: is the folder-level loss of the minority modality under a pooled centroid (a) the
image-text modality gap at work, and does the published post hoc fix (per-modality mean-centering,
GR-CLIP), applied before pooling, give one vector per folder that recovers what the per-modality
k-reps (c) recover with 5.27 vectors per folder?
Same set, cache, encoder, input rules and ground truth as sessions 5 to 7 (`results/session5.md`).
No download, no GPU, no new encoder. CPU only (MIG 1g.24gb pod, 3.4 vCPUs by cgroup; numpy 2.5.2,
scikit-learn 1.9.1), `DIRVEC_THREADS`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS` and `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS` set to 3.

Changes to `scripts/eval.py` (commits abe5965 and a9311de), defaults unchanged: `--calib` (fraction
of directories held out for the group means, stratified by image_frac bucket, `--calib-seed`
20260930), `--reps`, `--tag`; representations ab, ac, acb, cc, dc; the session 8 output section.
ab is the mean of the two per-group means (plain means of the uncentered child vectors, then
unit-normalised); a folder with one input group only gets that group's mean, which is a.

Run: `eval.py --model jina-embeddings-v4 --emb jina-embeddings-v4_s5 --manifest
data/manifest_s5.jsonl --dirs data/dirs_s5.jsonl --gt data/gt_structural_s5.jsonl --criterion s3
--calib 0.2 --reps a,b,c,d,ac,ab,acb,cc,dc --tag _s8`, 4 minutes. It was run twice: the first
run's header counted the 4,388 calibration-split queries as "dropped for lack of a vector"
(4,665 instead of 277); commit a9311de fixed the line, and the second run's output differs from the
first in that line only. The second run is the one below.

## Related work (copied from BRIEF.md, checked on 2026-09-30)

Related work checked on 2026-09-30. Li et al., "Closing the Modality Gap for Mixed Modality Search"
(NeurIPS 2025, arXiv 2507.19054): the image-text modality gap biases ranking in mixed-modality
corpora; their fix (GR-CLIP) subtracts per-modality mean embeddings post hoc; they evaluate single
items only (one image and one text) and name multi-image, multi-text documents as future work.
Grassucci et al., "Closing the Modality Gap Aligns Group-Wise Semantics" (ICLR 2026, arXiv
2601.18525): closing the gap helps group-wise tasks such as clustering. VectorVFS stores per-file
vectors only; "Directory-Aware Query and Maintenance in Vector Databases" (arXiv 2606.16903) uses
directories as path scopes, not as embedded objects. Federated search (resource selection) is the
classic relative of folder ranking and must be cited. Not read: arXiv 2609.33659 (rate limited).

## Hypothesis (copied verbatim from BRIEF.md, fixed before the first eval run)

H8: on the evaluation split of the session 5 set, the centered pooled centroid ac (one vector per
folder) recovers more than half of c's gain over the pooled centroid a in both session 3 primary
cells: for recall@5, (ac - a) - 0.5 * (c - a) > 0 in P1 (image queries in [0,.2) and [.2,.5)
folders) and in P2 (text-like queries in [.5,.8) and [.8,1] folders).
Kill: H8 is dead if, in either primary cell, the paired 95% bootstrap interval (1000 resamples
over directories) of (ac - a) - 0.5 * (c - a) does not lie entirely above zero. Report it plainly
either way.
Secondary, reported, no kill: ab and acb the same way; ac - a over all queries and in the two
majority-modality cells (a fix that wins minority queries by losing majority ones must show
there); cc - c and dc - d everywhere; the modality gap and the same-folder cosines before and after
centering; vectors per folder for every representation.


## Verdict

H8 is dead. The centered pooled centroid ac recovers almost exactly half of c's gain over a, not
more than half. (ac - a) - 0.5 * (c - a) at recall@5:

| cell | queries | dirs | a | c | ac | ac - a | c - a | statistic | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| P1 image in [0,.2)+[.2,.5) | 2235 | 1034 | 0.411 | 0.647 | 0.527 | +0.116 | +0.236 | -0.002 | [-0.023, +0.018] |
| P2 textlike in [.5,.8)+[.8,1] | 2597 | 1014 | 0.438 | 0.680 | 0.558 | +0.119 | +0.241 | -0.001 | [-0.020, +0.019] |

Both intervals contain zero; neither lies above it. ac recovers 49 percent of c's gain in both
cells.

## Summary

1. Split: 562 calibration directories ([0,.2) 92, [.2,.5) 201, [.5,.8) 207, [.8,1] 62), whose
   2,383 image-input and 2,796 text-input files give mu_img and mu_txt (norms 0.683 and 0.695).
   Evaluation split: 2,246 directories, 18,742 queries over 2,212 of them. All 2,808 directories
   ranked. On the evaluation split, c - a is +0.236 [+0.204, +0.269] and +0.241 [+0.213, +0.273]
   in the primary cells, as on the full set.
2. The gap is there and centering closes it: the distance between the mean image-input and mean
   text-input vector over the evaluation split falls from 0.361 to 0.072.
3. Centering does not bring a folder's two groups together. Mean cosine of same-folder file pairs:

   | vectors | img-img | txt-txt | img-txt |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|
   | before centering | 0.770 | 0.722 | 0.524 |
   | after centering | 0.568 | 0.462 | 0.205 |

   All three fall, the cross-group pairs most: before, a cross pair has 0.70 of the mean
   within-group cosine; after, 0.40. Closing the global gap removes the shared offset between the
   groups but leaves the within-folder separation, so a pooled vector still sits between two
   clusters. This is the reading of the numbers, not a tested claim.
4. Balancing, not centering, does most of the work. Secondary statistic (x - a) - 0.5 * (c - a),
   recall@5, with recall@5 of x:

   | x | vectors per folder | P1 recall@5 | P1 statistic | P2 recall@5 | P2 statistic |
   |---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
   | ac | 1.00 | 0.527 | -0.002 [-0.023, +0.018] | 0.558 | -0.001 [-0.020, +0.019] |
   | ab | 1.00 | 0.630 | +0.101 [+0.082, +0.119] | 0.661 | +0.102 [+0.087, +0.118] |
   | acb | 1.00 | 0.670 | +0.141 [+0.122, +0.159] | 0.696 | +0.137 [+0.121, +0.154] |
   | c | 5.27 | 0.647 | | 0.680 | |

   ab recovers 93 percent of c's gain in both cells; acb recovers 110 and 107 percent: in the
   minority cells one centered balanced vector per folder is above c (+0.023 and +0.016 recall@5,
   no interval computed for acb - c).
5. The balanced vectors pay for it in the majority-modality cells, the cost the brief asked to
   see. x - a, recall@5:

   | cell | ac - a | ab - a | acb - a | c - a |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | all queries | +0.035 [+0.031, +0.040] | +0.018 [+0.007, +0.030] | +0.039 [+0.028, +0.050] | +0.094 [+0.085, +0.104] |
   | image in [.5,.8)+[.8,1] | +0.009 [+0.002, +0.016] | -0.030 [-0.050, -0.012] | -0.025 [-0.044, -0.006] | +0.041 [+0.025, +0.059] |
   | textlike in [0,.2)+[.2,.5) | +0.003 [-0.003, +0.009] | -0.069 [-0.086, -0.050] | -0.048 [-0.063, -0.032] | +0.044 [+0.034, +0.056] |

   ac does not lose in the majority cells (it loses 0.010 and 0.016 only in the single-group-heavy
   buckets image [.8,1] and textlike [0,.2)). ab and acb lose 0.025 to 0.069 there and up to 0.149
   (ab, image [.8,1]). Over all queries every one-vector representation stays well below c
   (recall@5 ac 0.724, ab 0.707, acb 0.728, c 0.783).
6. Centering helps the multi-vector representations everywhere. cc - c: +0.017 [+0.015, +0.020]
   over all queries, +0.044 and +0.047 in P1 and P2, never significantly below zero in any cell.
   dc - d: +0.018 [+0.016, +0.021] over all queries, +0.043 and +0.037 in P1 and P2, interval
   above zero in every cell but image [.8,1] (+0.005 [-0.001, +0.010]). Recall@5 over all queries: cc 0.800, dc 0.801,
   c 0.783, d 0.783; recall@10 cc 0.832, dc 0.831, d 0.811. So the flat file-level walk d itself
   gains 0.02 from centering; the gain is not specific to folder vectors.
7. d - c on the evaluation split: +0.005 [-0.001, +0.013] (P1) and +0.012 [+0.007, +0.017] (P2).
